One of our major goals for Tachyon is making it accessible to users around the globe in their native language. While our live [Translation Status Dashboard](#/docs/Translations) tracks 36 supported languages, contributing translations previously required cloning the repository and editing JSON files manually.

Today, we are thrilled to announce that **Tachyon is now on Hosted Weblate!**

👉 **[Tachyon Webmail on Weblate](https://hosted.weblate.org/projects/tachyon-webmail/)**

---

## What is Weblate and Why Does It Matter?

[Weblate](https://weblate.org/) is a premier web-based localization platform for open-source projects. By integrating Tachyon with Weblate:

* **Zero Coding or Git Knowledge Needed**: Anyone can suggest or translate strings directly in a clean, visual browser interface.
* **Instant Direct Links**: Our [Translation Status Dashboard](#/docs/Translations) now links directly to each language's Weblate translation page (for example, [Danish on Weblate](https://hosted.weblate.org/translate/tachyon-webmail/-/da/)).
* **Automatic Pull Requests**: When you translate strings on Weblate, the system automatically batches changes and submits a Pull Request to the GitHub repository.
* **Quality & Consistency Checks**: Weblate automatically checks for missing formatting tags, trailing spaces, and interpolation variables to prevent syntax errors.

---

## How You Can Help

Whether you want to translate an entire language or just fix a single typo in your native tongue, every contribution makes a difference:

1. Visit our **[Translation Status Dashboard](#/docs/Translations)** to check your language's completion percentage.
2. Click **Translate** next to your language to jump directly into Weblate.
3. Translate or review a few sentences and submit!

Thank you to all community members and translators helping make Tachyon accessible worldwide!
